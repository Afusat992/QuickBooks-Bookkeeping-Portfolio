## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my practical experience with bookkeeping, transaction classification and financial record management using QuickBooks Online.

I created a fictional US service business, Northstar Creative Services LLC, to demonstrate how routine financial transactions are recorded, classified and maintained to support accurate accounting records.

The project covers customer invoices, supplier bills, operating expenses, loan repayments and adjusting journal entries.

All business information and transactions used in this demonstration are fictional.

## Business Scenario

Northstar Creative Services LLC is a fictional service business based in Texas, United States.

The company provides marketing, design and reporting services to small business clients.

The accounting records are maintained in US dollars using the accrual basis of accounting.

## Project Objectives

The objectives were to demonstrate:

1. Accurate recording and classification of financial transactions.
2. Customer invoicing and accounts receivable management.
3. Supplier bill processing and accounts payable management.
4. Proper recognition of operating expenses.
5. Correct accounting treatment of loan repayments.
6. Month end adjusting journal entries.
7. General ledger accuracy and financial reporting preparation.

## Accounting Activities Completed

### 1. Chart of Accounts

Established a structured chart of accounts covering assets, liabilities, equity, revenue and expenses.

### 2. Accounts Receivable

Recorded customer invoices totaling $25,500.

Customer receipts were applied against outstanding invoices to reduce accounts receivable without recognizing revenue twice.

### 3. Accounts Payable

Recorded supplier bills and payments.

Bills were recognized when the related expenses were incurred, while payments reduced outstanding liabilities.

### 4. Expense Classification

Recorded routine operating expenses, including rent, software subscriptions, office supplies, utilities, travel and payroll related expenses.

Transactions were classified according to their economic nature.

### 5. Loan Repayment

Recorded a $900 loan payment comprising:

* Loan principal: $800
* Interest expense: $100

The principal reduced the outstanding loan liability, while the interest was recognized as an expense.

### 6. Adjusting Journal Entries

Recorded a monthly depreciation adjustment of $200.

Also recorded an accrued professional service expense of $750 to recognize an expense incurred before receiving the supplier invoice.

## Accounting Principles Demonstrated

**Accrual Accounting:** Recognizing income and expenses in the appropriate accounting periods.

**Double Entry Bookkeeping:** Recording transactions using appropriate debit and credit entries.

**Accounts Receivable and Payable:** Maintaining accurate outstanding customer and supplier balances.

**Expense Recognition:** Distinguishing operating expenses from liability repayments.

**Adjusting Entries:** Recognizing depreciation and accrued expenses during the month end accounting process.
